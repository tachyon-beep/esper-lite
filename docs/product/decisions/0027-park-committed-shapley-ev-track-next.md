## Context

PDR-0026 scored the n=5 A/B null-not-informative (coverage-bound: paid
episodes 0.49%, k≥2 = 1.10% — the pre-registered floor hit exactly) with the
mechanism all-green, and put three next-leg options to the owner:
(a) park the term and start the queued EV/advantage-pathology track;
(b) a coverage-raising leg; (c) reshape the credit toward pre-fossil payment.

## Options

- **(a) Park** — term stays default-OFF, instrument banked as validated;
  EV-stabilization (esper-lite-f25b71c165) becomes the Now bet.
- **(b) Coverage-raising leg** — rejected for now: k≥2 co-fossilization is
  itself the target behavior; raising it by config fiat tests a different
  curriculum, not the term.
- **(c) Pre-fossil credit reshape** — explicitly unlicensed by a null under
  the prereg's asymmetric-null rule (PDR-0018 §7 does not fire).

## Call

**(a). The Committed-Shapley top-up is PARKED**: `shapley_synergy_scale`
stays 0.0 everywhere, the flag stays owner-gated, the validated instrument +
telemetry + scoring estimator are banked for reuse. **EV-stabilization
(esper-lite-f25b71c165) moves Next → Now.** The enablement-gate task
esper-lite-f22a1d48a7 closes — its deliverable (calibrate + A/B before any
scale > 0) is complete; the answer was "not yet, channel too narrow."

## Rationale

The EV track attacks the advantage-noise problem that plausibly suppresses
commitment in the first place. If it works, k≥2 co-fossilization may rise
organically — at which point a re-run becomes *informative* (≥10× coverage
consumes the asymmetric null, PDR-0026 reversal trigger) without ever having
distorted the curriculum to manufacture coverage. Parking is not killing:
the term survived every validity, safety, and anti-farming test it faced.

## Reversal triggers

- EV track lands AND fossilize/ep or k≥2-coalition frequency rises materially
  (≥3× the 1.1% baseline) on post-EV control runs → put the A/B re-run
  decision back to the owner (a ≥10× coverage re-run that is STILL null then
  counts against the term — PDR-0026).
- Owner explicitly reopens (b) or (c) — (c) additionally requires the
  informative-underperformance condition of PDR-0018 §7, which a parked term
  does not accrue.
- Before any n=10 MAGNITUDE claim on a future re-run: the τ null-player
  placebo leg (incl. GATE-placebo, addendum §5c) is a mandatory precondition.
