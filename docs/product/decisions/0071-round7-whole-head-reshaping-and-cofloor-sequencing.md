# PDR-0071 — Round-7 amendments: the dead-zone is WHOLE-HEAD; the floor fix is scoreable but not shippable alone; one primitive + λ-sweep

Date: 2026-07-13   Status: accepted (findings + sequencing correction within grant) — the floor-fix build + GPU experiment remain **owner-gated (proposed)**, unchanged from PDR-0070
Supersedes: **PDR-0070** (its two-build staged experiment + ship path are materially replaced). Amends the finding in **PDR-0069** (the "SET_ALPHA … LEARNED" clause is retracted; "two separable problems" becomes "co-requisite"). Driven by round-7 review (claude-prime + gpt-prime) + a zero-GPU `|H|` read on `stage2_on_longdiag/seed{41,42}`. Analysis: `docs/analysis/2026-07-13-decision-point-diagnosis.md` (round-7 §). ## Context
PDR-0069/0070 landed the floor gradient dead-zone as the commitment root cause and proposed a mixture-floor fix + a two-stage GPU experiment. Round-7 review sharpened the mechanism and caught a sequencing error that would ship a confident-fast-wrong policy.

## What does this buy, and how is that measured?  (REQUIRED — PDR-0068)
It prevents two concrete failure modes: (1) reading a floor-ceiling artifact as learning, and (2) shipping a gradient fix that, with the downstream credit channel dead, converts flat-commit into LOO-greedy commit collapse. Measured: `|H|=1` (whole-head constant output) in 67% (s41)/80% (s42) of HOLDING decisions; SET_ALPHA sits at the analytic cap `1−(n−1)f=0.55` in 48-61% of its choices (a ceiling, not a mode).

## The amendments
1. **The dead-zone is WHOLE-HEAD, not FOSS/PRUNE-specific.** At `|H|=1` (one op above floor) the output vector is a CONSTANT `[1−(n−1)f, f, …]` → zero gradient to EVERY op logit. `|H|=1` in **67-80%** of HOLDING decisions (both seeds). **RETRACT** PDR-0069/round-6 "SET_ALPHA … LEARNED": its 0.55 is the analytic cap (48-61% at exactly 0.55), not a learned preference. The op head is constant in the majority of decisions and learns only in the ~20-33% `|H|≥2` minority — a bigger defect and a better explanation for a structureless op head.
2. **The floor fix is SCOREABLE but NOT SHIPPABLE alone → `hindsight_credit`/transfer is a CO-REQUISITE.** With the downstream settlement inert (0.005%), unfreezing the gradient makes the policy optimise a myopic proxy (greedily fossilise high CURRENT LOO) with no downstream correction → plausibly LOO-greedy commit collapse (a new attractor, reached faster). The dead-zone may be an accidental SAFETY property. So PDR-0069's "two separable problems (A commit-learning / B transfer, unaffected)" is amended: they are separate ENGINEERING but a **joint shipping gate** — the floor fix does not ship until the transfer settlement carries signal.
3. **ONE primitive + λ-sweep, not two builds.** `q = (1−λ)·softmax(z) + λ·uniform(legal)` reproduces the current floor/cap at λ=0.6 and IS the no-floor condition at λ=0 (`∂q/∂z=(1−λ)·J_softmax`, never zero). Build one primitive, sweep λ∈{0, 0.3, 0.6}: λ=0 = necessity smoke, rest = fix. (gpt's `q_i=f+(1−nf)p_i` is an equivalent minimal form; smooth `P(non-WAIT)≥ε` is the long-term product primitive, after the causal pilot.) **Pre-register: λ=0 most likely collapses to SET_ALPHA (the current do-nothing attractor), NOT WAIT** — "didn't collapse to WAIT" ≠ "floor unnecessary."
4. **Gate caveat.** The −0.7→+8.2 commit-reward-vs-LOO is IMMEDIATE reward; advantage `A≈r+γV(s′)−V(s)` uses `V(s′)`, which is NOT op-independent (fossilising CONSUMES the seed → V(s′) falls for high LOO, partially cancelling r). Signal survives the 9-pt spread, but the re-run must LOG per-decision advantage. Phrasing: "a strong LOO-graded commit reward the op head usually cannot DIRECTLY learn from" (not "the reward was doing its job").
5. **Scope correction.** The floor does NOT retroactively erase the prior nulls — Objective-A HRA had its own validated total-fit failure; the Shapley A/B was genuinely coverage-bound. It is the leading PROXIMAL explanation for flat HOLDING preferences, not a universal cause.
6. **Re-run logging** must add per-head approx-KL (only aggregate `approx_kl` is logged — the "op-head KL biased to zero" free confirmation is currently unavailable).

## Reversal trigger
- If the λ=0 smoke shows NO collapse (to WAIT or SET_ALPHA) on genuine multi-choice states, the hard floor may be obsolete → a different, stronger conclusion.
- If a λ>0 differentiable-floor arm restores commit-selectivity AND terminal product guardrails hold AND the transfer settlement now carries signal, the co-requisite is satisfied and the fix ships. If commit-selectivity emerges but destructive-intervention / accuracy-contribution guardrails regress (the predicted LOO-greedy collapse), the fix is held pending the `hindsight_credit`/transfer work — confirming the co-requisite.
- If per-decision advantage-vs-LOO (logged in the re-run) turns out FLAT despite the graded immediate reward (V(s′) cancellation stronger than expected), the gate does NOT pass and the arm is unscoreable until the credit channel is fixed.
